Arena Asset Lab

Test benches for the arena game, before the real build. Everything here is drawn in code from pixel rectangles in the Claude Code mascot's style: flat colour, no outlines, chunky square eyes, four little legs. Colours, builds and gear are swapped at run time.

The four fighters

My own read on each model, kept to one silhouette change and one or two props each so they stay recognisable at 20 pixels wide in the colosseum.

Haiku · the scoutSmallest and quickest. A paper-white scarf and a sprout on its head: short poems, fast feet.
Sonnet · the knightThe mascot as it is, in a steel kettle helm. Balanced and dependable.
Opus · the sageTall and hooded, eyes that glow. Slow, deliberate, carries a staff until it loots a weapon.
Fable · the legendThe biggest frame, a laurel crown, a red cape and gold pauldrons.

Fifteen colours

Body colour is the agent's reasoning mode, so the two fighters in a duel almost always differ.

The crowd and the judges

Every match has its own judge, a separate sub-agent. Here a judge is a spectator in a white toga who stands up when the verdict file lands and gives the thumb. Down marks the loser and up marks the winner.

The colosseum

This plays back a real run's files. Fighters loot while their first solution is being written and equip when it lands. A round opens only after every fight in the previous one is over. Each attack, shield, heal, judge and death happens when its file was written, and the pace of the waves comes from the files' times. Drag the timeline to jump anywhere, as if you came back later. Click a fight or a body to open that match below. Hover anyone for details.
