Arena Asset Lab

Test benches for the arena game, before the real build. The sprites are drawn in code, in the Claude Code mascot's style: flat colour, no outlines, chunky square eyes, four little legs. Everything you read sits in solid panels under the game screens.

The four fighters

One build per model, each with a single silhouette change and a prop or two, so they stay recognisable at 20 pixels wide.

Haiku · the scoutSmallest and quickest. A paper-white scarf and a sprout on its head.
Sonnet · the knightThe mascot as it is, in a steel kettle helm. Balanced and dependable.
Opus · the sageTall and hooded, eyes that glow. Carries a staff until it loots a weapon.
Fable · the legendThe biggest frame, a laurel crown, a red cape and gold pauldrons.

Fifteen colours

Body colour is the agent's reasoning mode, so the two fighters in a duel almost always differ.

The crowd and the judges

Every match has its own judge, a separate sub-agent. The judge stands up in the crowd and holds up a placard showing the winner. A laurel wreath flies down onto the winner's head. Then the loser falls.

The colosseum

Live shows a run that is still going: you watch fights as their files land, and you can rewind and jump back to live. Finished is a completed run you can replay at any speed. Here, live is simulated by revealing a real run's files at their real pace. Click a fighter, a body or a row in the feed to open that fight below. Hover anyone for details.
